## A. Why the work takes time (25 min)

Each question starts from something observed and asks why. Keep asking why until the answer points to something that can be changed (the 5-whys approach). Where possible, let him show the answer on one of the chosen cases. After each answer, mark the cause it concerns in [Appendix 2](#appendix-2-draft-causes-to-confirm) as confirmed, changed or rejected.

**Creating or completing PO lines** (112 minutes in 35 cases; 54% picked up again later)

1. You often leave a PO and come back to it later. What are you usually waiting for: approval, information, the supplier, or a moment to send several POs together?
2. Which part of entering a PO takes the longest: finding the article, the supplier, the price, or typing the lines? Why that part?
3. Why are articles or suppliers sometimes missing in Exact, and who creates them?
4. When do you add lines to an existing PO instead of creating a new one, and why then?

**Request intake & validation** (94 minutes in 11 cases; median 6 minutes per case)

5. Which requests are most often incomplete or unclear, and from whom?
6. What information is usually missing, and why is it not included from the start?
7. Why do drawing sets from Engineering sometimes miss drawings? Is there a list of which drawings an item needs?
8. Is there a standard way to submit a request? If not, why not?

**Purchase-price control** (98 minutes in 19 cases; about 0.87 minutes per checked line)

9. Where do price differences usually come from: supplier price changes, outdated prices in Exact, order quantity, or mistakes?
10. Why are prices in Exact sometimes outdated? Who updates them, and when?
11. When Finance returns a case, what is missing from their note?
12. On 18 September a mismatch meant Johan had to approve again. How often does that happen, and could the check come before approval?
13. Does Exact offer any way to compare a supplier confirmation with the PO?

**Standard work**

14. In the observations, 58 of 98 cases (59%) were done in one go without a recorded problem. Does that match how much of your work you would call standard? What makes a case not standard?

**If time allows: maximalisatie** (from the methodology's [CTA-informed elicitation](../methodology/Phase_1_Current_Methodology.md#cta-informed-elicitation))

15. How do you look for other demand for the same supplier, and what makes combining worth it?
16. After that, what makes you send the order now or hold it, for example size, urgency or a minimum order value?
17. What makes you look at a held order again?

## Appendix 1: kinds of work to show Arno

Grouped as in part 4 of the notebook. **Problems** are marked with ⚠.

**Specification**
- K09 Establish or judge the requirement: decide what exactly is needed, which option, whether an item can replace another.
- K10 Search historical POs or records: look up earlier orders to identify or verify an item.

**Supplier selection**
- K13 Search for another supplier or better price.

**Ordering**
- K02 Create or complete a PO for a need: enter or amend PO lines in Exact.
- K03 Maximalisatie and hold-or-order decision: combine demand into an open PO for the same supplier, and decide to hold or order.
- K04 Check before ordering: check price, and delivery date where stated, before the PO is sent.
- K05 Send or forward the PO to the supplier.
- K07 Approval-related handling: forward an approved PO, or note that approval is awaited or needed again.
- K08 ⚠ Clarify missing or unclear request information.
- K11 ⚠ Retrieve or complete drawings needed for an order.
- K15 Adjust an existing PO on request.
- Purchase types handled the same way: K06 credit-card purchase, K25 service or transport purchase.

**Monitoring**
- K17 Check a supplier confirmation: compare it with the PO on price and delivery date, update Exact, attach it.
- K19 Follow up order status or delivery.

**Follow-up and evaluation**
- K21 Aftercare on a delivered order: wrong or incorrect items received.

**No fixed stage**
- K12 Article or supplier data in Exact: create or maintain article or supplier records.
- K28 Quotation or offer handling.

**Problems that can occur anywhere**
- K14 ⚠ Handle an unavailable item.
- K16 ⚠ Cancel a component or order line.
- K18 ⚠ Investigate a price difference.
- K20 ⚠ Finance-related issue, such as an unpaid invoice that blocks a supplier.
- K26 ⚠ Exact error or delay.

**Something that arrives**
- K01 Receive a request.
- K29 Question from a customer, Sales or Service.

**Work outside the purchasing activities**
- K22 Certification arrangements (Lloyd).
- K23 Logistics coordination.
- K24 Internal coordination with colleagues.
- K27 Read or organise purchasing email.
- K30 Administrative booking in Exact (*binnenboeken*).
- K31 Missing or attached certificate.
