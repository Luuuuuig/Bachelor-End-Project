# Dennis observation guide

Prepared for the tactical purchasing discovery and pattern evidence scheduled in the 11 September Gantt. This is a collection guide, not a record of completed observations.

## Purpose

Understand Dennis's purchasing tasks and their connections with Arno. Identify what information and decisions Dennis supplies, what work returns to him, and which patterns help explain operational purchasing workload. Logistics remains outside the thesis.

## Walk through real cases

Ask Dennis to show a recent routine purchase and a purchase that required additional investigation or a changed supplier or agreement. These are coverage prompts; do not label a case standard or non-standard before its characteristics are recorded.

1. What triggered this work, and what information was available at the start?
2. What did you do next? Show the relevant record or screen where access permits.
3. Who determined the required item or service and its specifications?
4. Was the supplier already fixed, or did you compare alternatives? What criteria mattered?
5. Which price, delivery or other terms were already agreed, and which required negotiation?
6. At what point could Arno proceed? What information did he receive, and through which route?
7. What questions or corrections came back? Trace the actual case before assigning a cause.
8. Which steps change when you or Arno covers for the other person?

`Case | Activity | Start | End | Volume | INT | DEC? | Result / short note`

Use `OTHER` with a concrete action description when a tactical task does not fit the existing codes. Apply Van Weele stages after the session.

Keep timed observations separate from employee estimates and retrospective walkthrough statements.
