# 4. Post-session enrichment

**Review date:** 18 September 2026. This is retrospective researcher enrichment. It applies the task-purpose framework to the preserved observations; it is not new buyer validation or a change to the live measurement method. [Source before this review](https://github.com/Luuuuuig/Bachelor-End-Project/blob/304726708216025a561cb7a35023eb0aace4335c/docs/measurement/Measure_Observation_2026-09-01.md).

The [31-task register](../process/Process_Cleaned_V1.5.md) supplies task descriptions. The [scope and classification addendum](Scope_and_Classification_Addendum_2026-09-14.md) supplies the later thesis boundary. The rebuilt 18 September framework assigns stages by the purpose of those tasks. Its rules are stated here so the classification can be reviewed without a separate Word file.

- Keep the seven live families **REQ, CLAR, DEC, PO, CHECK, SEND and OTHER**. Historical EXC remains source evidence and is excluded analytically. Original family labels, channel suffixes, timestamps, volumes, INT, DEC flags and dated corrections are unchanged.
- **Register status / confidence:** `Mapped` identifies supported registered tasks, `U` known work outside the register, `Mixed` registered and unregistered work, and `Unresolved` insufficient task evidence. `C` means directly supported, `P` provisional, and `?` unresolved. Clear observation recall does not establish an unrecorded task or control point.
- **Stage / confidence:** `VW1` specification, `VW2` supplier selection, `VW3` contracting, `VW4` ordering, `VW5` monitoring, `VW6` follow-up/evaluation. `UNKNOWN` retains unresolved purpose. `MULTI` requires evidence of inseparable stages. Task and stage confidence are independent.
- **Purpose rules:** establishing the required item, size or quantity supports specification; preparing or validating a defined order supports ordering; comparing and processing a supplier confirmation supports monitoring. A price request alone does not establish selection or contracting. Unknown pre/post-order CHECK work remains `UNKNOWN`. Article-master work remains stage-unresolved unless its purchasing purpose is supported.
- **Scope / quality:** classify each episode independently. Preserve EXC and demonstrated aftercare/logistics as excluded source evidence. Use `REVIEW_SCOPE` for unresolved or inseparable mixed scope. A neighbouring excluded episode does not determine the whole case's scope. Record-quality uncertainty is separate from stage uncertainty.
- **Time:** retain each observed interval once. Do not divide combined PO/MAX work, count DEC flags as extra time, infer approval actions, or reconstruct missing timestamps. `n/a` is missing or ineligible time, never zero. An unresolved stage can still contribute to eligible family time. Eligible minutes require INCLUDE, VALID and a recorded start/end pair.

The source row is the one-based position in the morning and afternoon observation tables in their written order. It identifies a record, not a unique PO. Case identity retains its observation date and any recorded earlier origin date.

This table replaces the earlier grouped enrichment. It retains its supported contextual clarifications while reviewing task ranges and adding separate stage, scope and timing judgments. The previous enrichment remains accessible in the linked source revision.
