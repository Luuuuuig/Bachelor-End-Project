## How to read the mapping

Use Van Weele to locate the purpose of work in the purchasing process. Retain the live work family to describe the observable action. The same CHECK or SEND family can occur in several stages. Add the stage after observation; preserve the original family, time and decision fields.

The six-stage model distinguishes tactical specification, supplier selection and contracting from operational ordering, monitoring and evaluation. The Dutch labels below follow Nevi. These are theoretical stages, not six newly observed company steps or exclusive job descriptions. Johan described role overlap; detailed Dennis evidence is still required.

## Six purchasing stages

| Code | Dutch stage | English meaning | Classification purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| VW1 | Specificeren | Specify the purchase need | Establish what is required and the requirements it must meet. |
| VW2 | Selecteren | Select a supplier | Compare and choose suppliers for a defined need. |
| VW3 | Contracteren | Negotiate and agree terms | Establish commercial terms and the supplier agreement. |
| VW4 | Bestellen | Prepare and place the order | Apply the purchasing need and existing agreements to order preparation and placement. |
| VW5 | Bewaken | Monitor the order | Check confirmation and fulfilment against the order and follow progress. |
| VW6 | Nazorg | Follow up and evaluate | Resolve subsequent issues and evaluate the completed purchase or supplier performance. |

Model basis: [Nevi, Het inkoopproces](https://nevi.nl/inkoop/inkoopkennis/inkoopproces); [Bäckstrand et al. (2019), section 3.2 and Figure 3](https://repub.eur.nl/pub/123697/Repub_123697_O-A.pdf), which reproduces Van Weele (2018, Figure 1.2, p. 9). The detailed local assignments below are this project's operationalization.

## Rules for analysis

1. Preserve the live family and its timing. Add `Van Weele stage`, `stage confidence`, `scope status` and a short reason in the derived dataset.
2. Use `MULTI` for several evidenced stages within one inseparable episode. Use `UNKNOWN` if the purpose is unclear. Never split or duplicate minutes to make a row fit the model.
3. Keep REQ timing conditional on actual intake work. Standalone DEC remains a tally; an embedded decision stays in the host episode's `DEC?` field.
4. Keep actor and stage separate. Arno can perform supplier-selection work; Dennis can perform operational work. Their observed workloads remain separate profiles.
5. Apply the logistics and EXC exclusion independently of the theoretical stage. A phase name does not override the agreed thesis scope.
6. Preserve system and supplier events as context without inventing buyer handling time.
