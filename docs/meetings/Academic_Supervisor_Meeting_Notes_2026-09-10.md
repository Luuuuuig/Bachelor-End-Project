## 3. Key Discussion Points

### Project context and thesis boundaries

The project context will be expanded in response to Johan's wishes. However, Yijie and the academic supervisor shared the view that improving the entire end-to-end supply-chain process, as they understood Johan's expectation, would exceed what is feasible for a bachelor student.

Logistics will not be included in the thesis for the following reasons:

- **Insufficient data and limited observation opportunities:** Mike's upcoming trip means Yijie will not have the opportunity to conduct further measurements or observations within logistics.
- **Difficulty tracking purchase orders across departments:** Observations are recorded under case identifiers such as `OBS-XX`, rather than using the PO number itself as the case identifier. Work concerning the same PO could therefore appear under different observation IDs, making it difficult to link logistics observations with observations of the operational buyer.
- **Extent of required revisions:** Including logistics would require substantial changes to the BEP wording. Keeping it outside the thesis was considered simpler.

### EXC time data

Yijie raised the possibility that measurement could identify EXC, described in the discussion as aftercare related to logistics, as the largest workload contributor.

The supervisor's direction was to exclude EXC time data during Measure from this point onward, given that logistics is outside consideration. The stated purpose is to prevent EXC from becoming the focal case.

### Dennis and data sufficiency

Dennis, the tactical buyer, will be included in the project because Johan specifically requested his involvement.

The academic supervisor stated that the quantity of measurement data collected for Dennis does not necessarily need to equal the quantity collected for the operational buyer. The requirement is to identify clear patterns in the relevant datasets. Data quantity can be considered sufficient when those patterns are clear, referring back to the discussion in the [3 September supervisor meeting note](https://github.com/Luuuuuig/Bachelor-End-Project/blob/main/docs/meetings/Academic_Supervisor_Meeting_Notes_2026-09-03.md).

### Two high-level workflow versions

An idea was discussed to prepare two versions of the high-level workflow:

| Intended use | Logistics coverage | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Version for Johan | Includes logistics | Show the broader workflow and acknowledge the logistics observations already conducted. |
| Version for the thesis | Excludes logistics | Reflect the agreed thesis boundary and keep the research scope manageable. |

This was discussed as an idea, rather than recorded as a confirmed action.

### Plan of Work and terminology

The Plan of Work and BEP wording need to reflect the revised scope. The wording of the current measurement categories also needs adjustment.

Because Johan uses Van Weele's purchasing-process model, the categories should be reviewed with the aim of matching them to that model.

## 4. Decisions Made

- Expand the project context and include Dennis's tactical purchasing work.
- Exclude logistics from the thesis.
- Exclude EXC time data during Measure so that EXC cannot become the focal case.
- Assess the sufficiency of Dennis's data through clear patterns, without requiring the same quantity of data as for the operational buyer.
- Adjust the Plan of Work, BEP wording, and measurement-category wording to reflect the revised scope and seek alignment with Van Weele's model.
