## 5. Operational interpretation of the Day-5 review

The sufficiency review should combine three forms of evidence:

1. **Coverage:** weekdays, dayparts, request channels, recurring activity families and relevant exception work have been represented sufficiently for the case study.
2. **Quantitative stability:** cumulative occurrence rates, active-time summaries and the ranking of important workload contributors are not changing materially when the latest day is added.
3. **Qualitative saturation:** new observation periods are no longer revealing materially new recurring activities, decisions, exceptions or case types.

“Fits a distribution” should not be interpreted as a requirement that every variable be normally distributed. Any distributional analysis must match the metric, sample size and data-generating process. It should support, not replace, the coverage, stability and saturation assessment.
