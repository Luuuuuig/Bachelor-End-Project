## Pilot observations about the observation method

This pilot showed that one purchasing case can be fragmented into several separate work episodes because of calls, colleague questions, drawing requirements and case switching.

The OBS-14/15 case is a clear example: forwarding the PO was not one continuous task. Work resumed several times before the required drawings were attached and the PO was finally forwarded.

The repeated case-number issue also showed that the observer needs to reconnect returning cases consistently during later baseline observations.

Uncertain observations, such as OBS-08, should remain explicitly uncertain rather than being reconstructed after the fact.

This note is a cleaned historical observation record only. Formal Task-ID coding, workload analysis and candidate interpretation should be performed separately according to the measurement protocol.
