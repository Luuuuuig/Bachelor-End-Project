# Workload Literature References

These references are relevant for defining and measuring workload in the BEP, especially for distinguishing objective task load from perceived mental workload.

1. Young, M. S., Brookhuis, K. A., Wickens, C. D., & Hancock, P. A. (2015). *State of science: Mental workload in ergonomics*. *Ergonomics, 58*(1), 1–17. https://doi.org/10.1080/00140139.2014.956151

2. Hart, S. G. (2006). *NASA-Task Load Index (NASA-TLX); 20 years later*. *Proceedings of the Human Factors and Ergonomics Society Annual Meeting, 50*(9), 904–908. https://doi.org/10.1177/154193120605000909

3. Longo, L., Wickens, C. D., Hancock, G., & Hancock, P. A. (2022). *Human mental workload: A survey and a novel inclusive definition*. *Frontiers in Psychology*.
