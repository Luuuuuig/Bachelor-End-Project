## Research structure and constructs

DMAIC structures improvement of an existing process: Define establishes scope and the AS-IS; Measure describes work; Analyze investigates causes and selects a feasible opportunity; Improve develops the artifact; Control specifies ownership, monitoring and safeguards ([de Mast and Lokkerbol, 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijpe.2012.05.035)). DSRM organizes problem identification, objectives, design/development, demonstration, evaluation and communication, principally within Improve ([Peffers et al., 2007](https://doi.org/10.2753/MIS0742-1222240302)). The stored 2006 conference paper is a different publication.

Workload means the **amount and difficulty of purchasing work** ([Bowling and Kirkendall, 2012](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119942849.ch13)). Frequency, volume, active processing time and repeated work describe amount; uncertainty, exceptions, problem solving and judgement supply qualitative difficulty evidence. Fast expert decisions may be difficult despite little recorded time. Expertise dependence concerns tacit cues and experience, not demonstrated mental workload.

[Spector and Jex (1998)](https://doi.org/10.1037/1076-8998.3.4.356) distinguish quantity/pace from organizational constraints such as interruptions, unavailable information and system or handoff problems. Their questionnaires are conceptual anchors, not administered instruments. Mental and perceived workload are not assessed. Heterogeneous indicators are not added into an unvalidated total-workload score. For comparable recurring tasks, `frequency × representative active processing time` may estimate capacity consumption, not total workload. Rework occurrence can signal process/quality problems while rework time adds work.

Purchasing quality is fulfilment of the requirements relevant to the selected activity, using the [literature register's ISO 9000 reference](../literature/README.md#generic-quality-definition) as a generic anchor. Quality is separate from workload; observed speed, interruptions or rework do not directly establish outcome quality.

## Observation rationale and operationalization

The baseline uses researcher-developed **structured continuous observation**, informed by published principles and the local process, not an instrument supplied by workload theory. [Mintzberg (1970)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-6486.1970.tb00484.x) provides a management-research precedent; [Fix et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pecinn.2022.100036) supports explicit operationalization and piloting. [Zheng et al.'s STAMP (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1136/amiajnl-2011-000083) informs reporting of categories, preparation, windows, dayparts, interruptions, transitions, simultaneous work and unavailable periods. [Lopetegui et al. (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbi.2014.02.017) distinguishes continuous episode observation from instantaneous work sampling. WOMBAT supplies structural analogues for timing, categories and context ([Westbrook and Ampt, 2009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijmedinf.2008.09.003); [Westbrook et al., 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3799166/)). Healthcare taxonomies and validation do not transfer automatically to purchasing.

The AS-IS register and 28 August pilot supplied local categories. Fragmented cases and impractical live Task-ID coding led to broad families: `REQ`, `CLAR`, `DEC`, `PO`, `CHECK`, `SEND`, `OTHER`; historical `EXC` remains traceable. Seven is a practical choice, not a validated category count. Version 1.1 governed 31 August; 1 September transcription follows v1.2. [Protocol v1.3](../evidence/protocols/Measurement_Protocol_v1.3.md), effective 2 September, retains v1.2 live fields; the [dated addendum](../evidence/protocols/Scope_and_Classification_Addendum_2026-09-14.md) governs subsequent scope and timing changes. Method rationale was documented on 3 September; the whole later framework was not predefined before collection.

| Recorded evidence | Operational meaning and limit |
|---|---|
| Family, occurrences, known observation exposure | Work mix and sampled-window recurrence; not a representative weekly rate |
| Start/end, reliable active minutes, case/line volume | Time requirements and case size; tallies and passive waiting supply no active duration |
| INT, segmented/resumed episodes | Fragmentation and constraints; segment count alone does not establish an interruption |
| Origin/channel, clarification and rework notes | Arrival context and additional/obstructed work; no automatic workload or quality score |
| DEC?, J, EXP, reasoning | Supported judgement/expertise cues; no inferred invisible cognition or validated cognitive scale |
| Derived kind, stage, analytical activity | Purchasing purpose; classification adds no observation or duration |
| MISS, confidence, unresolved labels | Measurement uncertainty; traceability does not repair missing evidence |

For judgement-heavy shortlisted work, [ACTA-informed questioning (Militello and Hutton, 1998)](https://doi.org/10.1080/001401398186108) asks about cues, decisions, difficulty, missing information, alternatives and prior experience. Loose notes are exploratory evidence, not a reproducible difficulty scale. This is not automatically a full CTA study. MAX/order-hold elicitation and advice/reliance concepts are conditional on the chosen task; neither creates a separate required study.

## Two-stage candidate selection

**Stage A is non-compensable.** Pass, Redesign or Reject addresses: (1) permitted data/tool use; (2) representative accessible prototype/test inputs; (3) reproducible reference and manual baseline; (4) detectable/reversible critical errors, human review, audit trail and fallback; (5) development/evaluation within time, access and participant constraints; (6) meaningful AI value beyond simpler deterministic rules. Missing evidence is unresolved. Buyer acceptance of the specific support remains material; full-automation objections do not establish acceptance of bounded support.

If no candidate passes, first investigate bounded redesign, reduced autonomy or better data. If none becomes responsible and evaluable, report failed gates and apply [Project's non-AI exception rule](PROJECT.md#purpose-and-scope). High scores never compensate for failed gates.

**Stage B** compares observed burden, business value beyond counted time, AI–task fit, input readiness, repetition/standardisation, build feasibility and evaluation feasibility. These are project judgements informed by [Goodhue and Thompson (1995)](https://doi.org/10.2307/249689), [Belton and Stewart (2002)](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4615-1495-4) and [DCLG (2009)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/multi-criteria-analysis-manual-for-making-government-policy), not literature-prescribed criteria or cut-offs. Count current burden, not forecast/achieved savings. Separate recurrence from counted time, business consequences from wages, and input preparation from build effort and test arrangements. Discrepancy amounts are not losses or recoverable benefits; downstream benefits require an implemented handoff.

The [selection notebook's criteria section](../Focal_Case_Selection_2026-10-07.ipynb#criteria) retains exact 1/3/5 anchors. Scores 2/4 need an evidenced intermediate rationale; missing evidence stays unresolved. A score of 1 cannot override a failed gate. The original business-value score-5 anchor requires company-supervisor confirmation. The 9 October proposed substitutions/clarifications remain unadopted. Changed anchors require documented academic review, reconsidered scores and swing weights, and recalculation; general difficulty claims cannot justify invented minute splits or upgrades.

Swing weights value the move from anchor 1 to 5: `w_i = swing_i / Σswings`. The total is `Σ(w_i × score_i/5)`, displayed times 100; its range is 20–100, a preference score, not savings or success probability. The [7 October record](../evidence/decisions/Swing_Weights_Yijie_2026-10-07.md) discloses that Yijie adopted the worked example unchanged before scoring but knew workload figures. The 8 October meeting agreed review, not final weights or a procedure. Independent supervisor elicitation, averaging and discussion above 10 raw swing points remain proposals; independence cannot be claimed after exposure to rankings.

Check agreed and equal weights, each weight ±20% with renormalization, disputed scores ±1 and combined uncertainty, following the sensitivity rationale of [Triantaphyllou and Sánchez (1997)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-5915.1997.tb01306.x). If the winner changes, report instability and seek evidence or retain a shortlist. Double-counting safeguards do not prove preference independence.

## Separate focal evaluation

Success requires **both** a meaningful improvement on a justified workload endpoint and a passing quality guardrail. After focal selection/manual-baseline review, freeze eligible cases, comparator, endpoint, smallest worthwhile improvement `Δ`, quality rubric/reference, critical errors, minor-error margin `δ`, sampling and analysis before prototype development/formal testing. The process owner sets operational thresholds; the academic supervisor validates the research rule. Separate development and held-out cases where feasible.

If time is the endpoint, include reading, searching, entry, review, correction and required handoffs; report passive waiting/unrelated interruptions separately and include eligible cases where work is prevented. Limit conclusions to supported workload dimensions and report affected Dennis work separately. Other indicators can describe actions, switching, rework, throughput and qualitative difficulty without aggregation.

Assess the **final human-approved outcome** against a trusted reference/adjudicator. Default quality protection is zero observed critical errors and final correctness no more than `δ` below the matched manual baseline; critical-error `δ = 0`, and any minor-error margin needs explicit process-owner acceptance. A small sample can pass the observed-case guardrail without establishing statistical non-inferiority.

Measures follow the problem: decision support may assess benchmark quality, constraints, consistency and overrides; detection may assess accuracy, precision/recall and false positives/negatives; exception routing may assess classification, escapes, output accuracy, review/correction burden, remaining judgement demands and eligible automation share. Historical/standardized-case benefits remain potential or estimated, not realized deployment savings. Source access, citation verification and reading completion remain distinct in the [literature register](../literature/README.md).
