# Internship Notes — 24 August 2026

## Work completed

The main work on this day was methodological rather than additional process shadowing.

- Reviewed the current AS-IS process and task register as the basis for structured Measure-phase observation.
- Worked on the design of a practical live observation protocol for shadowing the operational buyer.

## Literature / construct work

A major methodological question was how **workload** should be defined and measured in a way that is defensible for the BEP.

Work therefore focused on checking whether the existing mental-workload framing was sufficient for the broader company problem and on identifying literature that could support the measurement logic.

At this stage the workload framework was still being refined; the key concern was to avoid equating operational buyer workload with processing time alone.
