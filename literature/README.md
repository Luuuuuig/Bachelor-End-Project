# Literature package: AI-supported procurement order monitoring

This folder accompanies `Bachelor_End_Project_Research_Notes.docx`.

## Reading order

1. Fernandez et al. (2016), *Framework for modelling and simulating the supply process monitoring to detect and predict disruptive events*. Directly relevant to monitoring and early detection. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compind.2016.04.002)
2. Xu (2010), *A Web-based system for proactive management of supply exceptions*. Directly relevant to proactive exception handling. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmsy.2010.11.003)
3. Flechsig, Anslinger, and Lasch (2022), *Robotic Process Automation in purchasing and supply management*. Direct procurement-automation evidence. [Open-access DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pursup.2021.100718)
4. Schemmer et al. (2023), *Appropriate Reliance on AI Advice*. Core human-reliance measurement. PDF attached as `open-access/01-appropriate-reliance-ai-advice.pdf`. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3581641.3584066)
5. Peffers et al. (2007), *A Design Science Research Methodology for Information Systems Research*. Core artifact-building methodology. An accessible process paper by the authors is attached as `open-access/05-design-science-research-process.pdf`; cite the [original DSRM article](https://doi.org/10.2753/MIS0742-1222240302).
6. Amershi et al. (2019), *Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction*. Design principles for feedback, control, correction, and failures. PDF attached as `open-access/03-guidelines-human-ai-interaction.pdf`. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3290605.3300233)
7. Bansal et al. (2021), *Does the Whole Exceed its Parts?* Human-AI complementary performance. PDF attached as `open-access/02-human-ai-complementary-team-performance.pdf`. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3411764.3445717)
8. Syed et al. (2020), *Robotic Process Automation: Contemporary themes and challenges*. Automation risks and implementation issues. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compind.2019.103162)
9. van der Aalst (2012), *Process Mining: Overview and Opportunities*. Useful when event-log data are available. PDF attached as `open-access/04-process-mining-overview-opportunities.pdf`. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/2229156.2229157)
10. Bonaccio and Dalal (2006), *Advice taking and decision-making*. Judge-Advisor System experiment design. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obhdp.2006.07.001)
11. Goodhue and Thompson (1995), *Task-Technology Fit and Individual Performance*. Practical fit between agent capabilities and buyer tasks. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2307/249689)
12. Lee and See (2004), *Trust in Automation*. Foundational appropriate-reliance theory. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1518/hfes.46.1.50_30392)
13. Dietvorst et al. (2015), *Algorithm Aversion*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0000033)
14. Logg et al. (2019), *Algorithm Appreciation*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obhdp.2018.12.005)
15. DeLone and McLean (2003), *Information Systems Success Model: A Ten-Year Update*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/07421222.2003.11045748)
