## 6. What this means

This is a **retrospective, literature-informed assessment**, not a pre-registered test: the rules were proposed on 30 September after an informal look at the data, and the literature was consulted afterwards.

**Main pattern without EXC (view B).** PO and CLAR are the two largest activity codes whichever day is left out, but their order flips when 1 Sep or 8 Sep is left out, so the data show which two codes lead, not which of the two is larger. Ordering is the largest stage on every day. Adding day 5 moved no code share and no stage share by 5 points or more.

**With EXC (view A).** EXC ranges from 4% to 36% of a day's recorded minutes, so the top pair changes when 1 Sep or 8 Sep is left out. The stage share of Not applicable changes by +7.3 points on day 5, mainly because of the 42-minute OBS-16 episode (stage Not applicable), which Arno described as unusual. Ordering is still the largest stage on every day.

**Rates and CHECK.** Rates per observed hour are reported with their daily range, without a pass/fail rule. CHECK varies most (1 to 8 rows per session). Minutes per line stays exploratory: 0.87 pooled over 15 segments in 14 cases (median 1.00, range 0.38–4.00).

**Unrecorded time.** 150 of 892 net observed minutes have no recorded activity. Shares describe recorded work, not the whole observed time.

**Saturation.** Day 5 showed no new activity code, stage or request channel. New kinds of work per day were 14, 9, 3, 5 and 0; the later days mainly added detail inside kinds already seen, such as unpaid invoices blocking checks, approval repeated after a mismatch and incomplete drawing sets. 7 rows (24 minutes) on 23 September could not be classified.

**Coverage.** No Thursday, no measured time before 10:30 or after 15:00, and 8 September has a morning block only. Because the main pattern without EXC is consistent across the five days, these are documented limitations rather than gaps that change the result, following the supervisor's advice of 17 September.

**Conclusion.** The evidence supports pausing broad observation and starting Analyze for Arno. Conclusions that depend on the EXC decision or on the Dennis comparison stay provisional until the supervisor discussion.
